# Dog Wif Shiesty asset prompts

All three assets were made with Codex's built-in image generation tool. They are original artwork and intentionally avoid reproducing Dogwifhat or any existing meme photograph.

## Logo and favicon source

> Create an original square meme-token logo: a cute cream-and-charcoal scruffy terrier-mix, head and upper body centered, wearing a snug black knitted balaclava with crisp electric-neon-yellow piping around the face opening. Put the dog against a solid electric neon-yellow background. Use lo-fi direct-flash editorial photography, tactile fur and knit texture, confident deadpan expression, bold centered silhouette, and enough negative space to crop cleanly as a circular favicon. No hat, text, logos, trademarks, weapons, aggression, or recreation of an existing meme dog.

Saved as `public/shiesty-logo.png`, with favicon and Apple touch-icon crops derived from it.

## Forty-dog hero wall

> Create one original wide photographic contact sheet showing exactly 40 distinct cute dogs in a strict 8-column by 5-row grid. Every cell has one different dog portrait wearing a visibly different black, charcoal, off-white, or electric-neon-yellow balaclava. Blend early-internet meme collage, CCTV scanlines, photocopy grit, direct-flash photography, and abstract chain-native terminal geometry. Use thin off-white and neon-yellow dividers. Keep every dog charming and non-threatening. No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, weapons, people, duplicated dogs, missing cells, or imitation of Dogwifhat or any existing meme photo.

Saved as `public/shiesty-dog-wall.png`.

## Social preview

> Create a 1200 x 630 original black-and-neon-yellow editorial social card. Place the original scruffy dog in a black balaclava with neon-yellow piping on the right, with a subtle tiled masked-dog contact sheet and abstract terminal texture behind it. Use only this exact text: `DOG WIF SHIESTY`, `$SHIESTY`, and `IN THE HOOD, WE WEAR SHIESTYS.` Make the title large, condensed, and highly legible. No other text, logos, trademarks, threatening imagery, or imitation of an existing meme photo.

Saved as `public/og.png`.
